Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the JamSlate Android app handles. JamSlate is developed by Digital Slate Database.

1. No account required

JamSlate has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version of JamSlate shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 25.5.0) and manages consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (version 4.0.0).

Each time the app starts, it updates consent information through UMP and shows a consent message when one is required for your region. JamSlate requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. When privacy options are required, the app shows an "Ad privacy choices" button in About where you can review or change your choices.

According to Google's data disclosure, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Google processes this information under Google's Privacy Policy.

JamSlate does not run its own analytics.

JamSlate offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove ads". Google Play handles the payment: the developer never receives your payment details, and the app only learns whether the purchase exists on your Google account. If you own it, ads are turned off and the ads SDK is not started.

3. Information sent when you search for a song

Song search is optional: nothing is sent unless you use it. When you search, the app sends the text you type, over HTTPS, to MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) to find matching recordings, and the identifiers of those recordings to AcousticBrainz (acousticbrainz.org) to read the tempo and key measured from their audio. These requests carry no account, name or advertising ID from JamSlate, but, like any website, each service can see your IP address and handles it under its own privacy policy. JamSlate does not send your choices to the developer, and the band works fully offline.

4. Information on your device

The band is generated on your phone. Your choices (style, key, tempo, instrument levels) are used only while the app runs. The sound library is copied into the app's private storage the first time it starts, so playing needs no internet. JamSlate does not use your microphone, location or contacts. On Android 13 and later it asks for notification permission only so the music can keep playing, with a Stop button, while the screen is off; if you refuse, playback still works but Android may not show that notification.

5. Children

JamSlate is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. JamSlate keeps no personal data on any server of its own. For advertising data, use the "Ad privacy choices" button or Google's tools. You can also contact us with any request.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: gameslate@proton.me.
