Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the SongSlate Android app handles. SongSlate is developed by Digital Slate Database.

1. No account required

SongSlate has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data.

2. Advertising and consent

The free version of SongSlate shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 25.5.0) and manages consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (version 4.0.0).

Each time the app starts, it updates consent information through UMP and shows a consent message when one is required for your region. SongSlate requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. When privacy options are required, the app shows an "Ad privacy choices" button in About where you can review or change your choices.

According to Google's data disclosure, the Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Google processes this information under Google's Privacy Policy.

SongSlate does not run its own analytics.

SongSlate offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove ads". Google Play handles the payment: the developer never receives your payment details, and the app only learns whether the purchase exists on your Google account. If you own it, ads are turned off and the ads SDK is not started.

3. Microphone and recordings

SongSlate uses the microphone only while you record a take, and only after you allow the microphone permission. A recording is saved as a file inside the app on your phone. It is never uploaded, and the developer never receives it. If you deny the permission, everything else in the app still works.

SongSlate has no song search and sends no information about your songs anywhere. The internet is used only by the advertising described above.

4. Information on your device

Your songs (titles, chords and words), your recordings and your mix settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. You can remove them by deleting a song, clearing the app data or uninstalling the app. The sound library is copied into the app's private storage the first time it starts. If Android's backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy your songs (titles, chords and words) to your Google account; recordings and the sound library are not included in that backup. When you export, the file is created on your phone and is shared only if you choose an app in Android's share menu. On Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission only so that the music can keep playing, with a Stop button, while the screen is off.

5. Children

SongSlate is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. SongSlate keeps no personal data on any server of its own. For advertising data, use the "Ad privacy choices" button or Google's tools. You can also contact us with any request.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: digitalslate@tuta.com.
