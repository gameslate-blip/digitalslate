Privacy policy

Your data, clearly explained.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the BookSlate Android app handles. BookSlate is developed by Digital Slate Database (Raphael Lira).

1. No account required

BookSlate has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data.

2. Advertising and analytics

BookSlate does not show ads and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Information sent to Open Library

To find books and suggestions, the app sends requests over HTTPS to Open Library (openlibrary.org), including the text you type when searching and public book or subject identifiers. Book covers are loaded from covers.openlibrary.org. Like any website, Open Library can see your IP address and handles it under its own privacy policy. BookSlate does not send your list of books to the developer, and does not use your location, contacts or any other personal data. The app requests only the Internet permission.

4. Information on your device

Your language choice and the books you add are used on your device to produce suggestions. If a future version saves them, they will stay in private app storage on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Android device backup may include app data.

5. Children

BookSlate is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Because the developer does not receive or store your personal data, there is none to access or delete on our side. You may still contact us with any request, for example under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: gameslate@proton.me.
