How it works

Simple on purpose.

1 · Add

Your books

Search and add up to 15 books you've read.

2 · Match

Smart suggestions

BookSlate looks at the subjects and popularity of what you read to find books you haven't.

3 · Discover

3 picks

Get three focused suggestions, plus a Home tab with categories, trending and new releases.

BookSlate does not require an account. Book data and covers come from Open Library. BookSlate is independent and not affiliated with Open Library or the Internet Archive.
